Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. Understanding the core concepts of AI is essential before diving into a course. You will typically cover:


  Machine Learning
  Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  Computer Vision
  Robotics
  Neural Networks
  Deep Learning
